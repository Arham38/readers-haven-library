Library Membership Plans

Choose an annual membership tier that fits your reading habits. All plans include free access to reading halls and digital catalogues.

Student Plan

PKR 1,500 / Year

  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 3 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 14 days (renewable once)
  • Requirement: Valid university or school ID card

Adult Standard Plan

PKR 3,000 / Year

  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 5 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 21 days
  • Requirement: CNIC copy and proof of residence

Family Plan

PKR 5,500 / Year

  • Borrowing Limit: Up to 10 books across 4 family cards
  • Loan Period: 30 days (includes Children’s Corner access)
  • Requirement: Primary applicant CNIC

Late-Return & Replacement Rules

  • A grace period of 2 days is given after the due date.
  • Late returns are charged at PKR 30 per book per day.
  • Borrowing privileges are paused if unpaid fines exceed PKR 500.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced with the same edition or paid at current market price plus a PKR 200 processing fee.
