Library Services

Everything you can do at Readers' Haven besides borrowing books. Timings are for Monday to Saturday unless stated.

Catalogue Search

Available: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM

  1. Use the catalogue terminal near the entrance.
  2. Search by title, author, or subject.
  3. Note the shelf code shown in the result.
  4. Ask the front desk if you cannot find the book.

E-Books

Available: 24 hours online for members

  1. Log in with your membership number.
  2. Browse the e-book shelf and pick a title.
  3. Borrow it for up to 14 days.
  4. Read on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Study Room

Available: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, 2-hour slots

  1. Book a slot at the front desk, one day ahead.
  2. Show your membership card at check-in.
  3. Keep noise low and leave the room tidy.
  4. Slots are released after 15 minutes if you do not arrive.

Printing and Scanning

Available: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

  1. Bring your file on a USB drive or email it to the desk.
  2. Tell the staff the number of pages and colour or black and white.
  3. Pay at the desk (PKR 10 per black and white page).
  4. Collect your prints or scanned copy by email.

Wi-Fi

Available: during all opening hours

  1. Connect to the network named ReadersHaven-Guest.
  2. Ask the front desk for today's password.
  3. Use it for study and research only.
